PRODUCT TILE ARTWORK — IMAGE GENERATION PROMPTS
Kan Coffee House  ·  44 drinks  ·  5 batches of 10
================================================================================

Sibling of category-image-prompts.txt, for the other grain: one picture per
PRODUCT rather than per category. Upload them at Inventory -> product ->
Product picture (admin only).


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
0. READ THIS BEFORE YOU START
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

THREE THINGS RUIN A BATCH, AND ALL THREE ARE EASY TO WALK INTO.

  1. A BACKGROUND. The app CANNOT remove one. The upload will warn you, and the
     tile will show a rectangular block sitting on a colour gradient. You need a
     real cut-out with an alpha channel.
       - OpenAI: gpt-image-1 with background: "transparent". In the ChatGPT app,
         ask for it in words: "transparent background PNG, no backdrop".
       - DALL-E 3 CANNOT produce alpha at all. Asking it for "no background"
         gets you a white rectangle that looks fine until you upload it.

  2. A CONTACT SHEET. Asking for "10 images" in one message often returns ONE
     image containing ten drinks in a grid. That is useless here — each product
     needs its own file. Say it explicitly: one image per prompt, separately.

  3. DRIFT BETWEEN BATCHES. These are seen ten-at-once in a grid, so one
     different cup or one brighter light is immediately obvious. Do all five
     batches in the SAME conversation so the model can see what it already made,
     and paste the STYLE BLOCK verbatim at the top of every batch even so.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. SIZE AND FORMAT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Generate at:   1024 x 1024, square (1:1)
  Ship at:       whatever you like — the app normalises it
  Transparency:  REQUIRED. PNG with alpha.

You do not need to resize or crop anything. src/imageNormalize.js trims to the
subject's alpha bounding box, scales it to a consistent size and centres it on a
512x512 transparent canvas, so a 4000px render and a 200px icon land on the shelf
at the same visual weight. That trim is also why a generous even margin in the
source costs nothing.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. THE STYLE BLOCK — paste this at the top of EVERY batch, unchanged
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

You are producing a consistent SET of product cut-outs for a coffee shop's
point-of-sale screen. Consistency across the whole set matters more than any
single image: they are seen ten at a time in a grid, so one odd angle, one
different cup or one brighter light is immediately obvious.

Apply ALL of the following to every image:

- Output a PNG with a FULLY TRANSPARENT background. No backdrop, no surface, no
  table, no gradient, no white fill. A true cut-out.
- One single vessel per image, centred, filling most of the frame with an even
  margin around it.
- Identical camera for every image: three-quarter view from about 20 degrees
  above the rim. Do not vary the angle between drinks.
- Soft, even studio lighting from the upper left. No harsh highlights, no
  blown-out speculars.
- NO drop shadow, NO reflection, NO contact shadow baked into the image. The
  app adds its own shadow; a second one looks wrong.
- NO text, NO logos, NO branding, NO labels, NO packaging, NO hands, NO saucers,
  NO spoons, NO straws, NO napkins, NO background props. The vessel and the
  drink only, plus any garnish named in the prompt.
- Hot drinks: warm stone-grey ceramic. Cold drinks: plain clear glass.
  Do NOT use pure white ceramic — the tile can sit on a light background and a
  white cup loses its edges against it. Let the drink itself carry the colour.
- Photorealistic and appetising. Not illustration, not cartoon, not 3D render.
- Square 1:1.

Generate these ONE AT A TIME — one image per numbered prompt. Do NOT combine
them into a grid, contact sheet, collage or single composite image.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. FILENAMES AND WHY THEY CARRY THE SKU
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Save each file under the name given. The SKU prefix is not decoration:

THREE DRINKS EXIST TWICE ON THIS MENU, HOT AND COLD, AT DIFFERENT PRICES, AND
TWO OF THEM HAVE IDENTICAL NAMES.

    Spanish Latte      KC-HC-10 (hot, 3.75)   KC-CC-06 (iced, 4.00)
    Caramel Macchiato  KC-HC-11 (hot, 3.75)   KC-CC-07 (iced, 4.00)
    Mocha              KC-HC-09 (hot, 3.75)   KC-CC-05 (iced, 4.00)

Match uploads by SKU, never by name, or the hot one gets the iced picture and
nobody notices until a customer does.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. AFTER GENERATING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

CHECK THE ALPHA BEFORE YOU UPLOAD 44 FILES. Open one on a dark background and
one on a white background. If you can see a rectangle, it has no alpha and every
tile will show that rectangle — regenerate rather than upload.

Then: Inventory -> tap the product -> Product picture -> Choose image -> Save
picture. Admin only. The upload warns you if the image has no transparency, so
that warning is the second line of defence, not the first.

To replace one later, upload again — it overwrites. To go back to the category
artwork, use Remove.
